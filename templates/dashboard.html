Competitive landscape — Connecticut
Of the institutions in Connecticut, the University of New Haven is one of the few offering a dedicated graduate AI program combined with an AI in Cybersecurity specialization. The broader competitive set remains limited at the graduate AI level in CT, while New York and Massachusetts host significantly more established programs at peer-ranked universities. This represents a near-term window of differentiation for UNH — though market saturation at the graduate AI level is expected to accelerate regionally as institutions respond to employer demand through 2026 and beyond. Note: Program details marked "Pending" require manual verification via each institution's graduate catalog. Update data/raw/institutions_programs_2024.csv with confirmed data.
